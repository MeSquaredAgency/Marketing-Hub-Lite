## Who we are

Me² (Me Squared). Back-office teams and websites for established businesses. Each client gets their own trained, dedicated person, with Me² specialists in admin, finance, customer service and marketing behind them, and AI agents built around how the client already works. Everything is checked before it goes out; the person reports back like part of the client's team. We design and build the website out front, too. Team in Phnom Penh; clients in Australia.

## Voice

- Calm, specific, unhurried. We explain how something works rather than sell it.
- Write to the owner of an established business who is tired of doing admin at 10pm. Respect their time.
- Concrete nouns: inbox, invoices, data entry, the website. No "solutions", "synergy", "leverage", "unlock".
- Admit the model's shape honestly: the team is in Phnom Penh, the work is checked, the owner stays in control.
- No emoji in LinkedIn posts. One, at most, elsewhere.
- Hypothetical examples are labelled ("Say a plumber with four vans...").
