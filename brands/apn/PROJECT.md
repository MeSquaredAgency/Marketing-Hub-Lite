## Voice

- Plain, confident, local. We know these suburbs. Short sentences.
- First person plural ("we", "our team"). Name the agent on listing posts.
- No hype words: no "stunning", "dream home", "won't last", "must see". Say what the property is and who it suits.
- Property management posts speak to landlords and tenants as adults: what we do, what it costs them in time, what to expect.
- Australian English. Suburb names in full. Prices as "$650,000" not "$650k" on tiles; "$650k" acceptable in captions.
- One call to action per post: book an inspection, request an appraisal, call the office.

## Compliance rules (summary; the full checklist is in the skill)

1. **Legal line on every asset.** `Adelaide Property Network | RLA 255336` on every tile, caption, ad, video description and end card. The templates carry it; do not remove or restyle it.
2. **One vendor per post.** Never combine properties from different vendors in one tile, carousel or caption.
3. **Price and status accuracy.** Prices match the listing and the agency agreement. "Sold" or "Leased" only when true. Sale prices only with vendor agreement.
4. **Tenant privacy.** No tenant names, no tenanted interiors without consent, no arrears or disputes.
5. **Consent.** Every person pictured has an Assets row with consent recorded.
6. **Facts.** Market statistics and PM figures come from the Facts tab, dated within 90 days.
7. **Licensed agents only** named on posts; the team list below is the register.
