Say a plumber with four vans gets 60 emails a day. Quotes, supplier invoices, a tenant chasing a job time.

A Me² person reads them in the morning, answers the ones that have an answer, drafts the ones that need the owner, and logs the invoices. A finance specialist checks the invoices before anything is paid.

The owner sees three emails instead of sixty. That is the whole idea.
